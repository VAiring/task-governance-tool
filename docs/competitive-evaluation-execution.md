For this first six-group cohort only, the later explicit user approval replaces

Record
execution, measurements and results in the experiment directory. At closure

## E3 — Report And Stop

E3 follows E2, or an explicitly documented budget cutoff. Produce a comparison
with individual n=1 observations, raw usage and reference-index definitions,
missingness, preparation/evaluation/human cost, and limitations. Keep old runs
separate. Do not infer general superiority, causality or restart benefits from
this ordinary-work case. Identify whether further comparison would answer a
useful question without starting it. Verify source-to-report consistency and
obtain two independent Tier 2 reviews with no blocking findings.
