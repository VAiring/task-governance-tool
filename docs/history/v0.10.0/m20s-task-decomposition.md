## Attempted Pair And Eligibility

TG-M20S.2 started only the first authorized replacement pair,
`sp_user_expansion_alternate`. It used one fresh broad arm and one fresh
bounded arm in isolated repository roots. Both arms terminalized and all four
required records—one machine measurement and one observer attestation per
arm—were eligible. The terminal corpus therefore contained four records from
one attempted pair.

The two later fixed replacement pairs were never launched. They are
unavailable conceptual slots after the positive stop, not failed trials,
excluded samples, or evidence against the result.

## Frozen Qualification Result

Every bounded-arm episode attested each required independence field as
`yes`. The broad arm contained non-independent commit/completion boundaries,
so the paired attestations also supported the intended contrast without
retaining the per-episode map.

That exact frozen improvement predicate was sufficient to qualify the pair.
Supporting footprint, invocation, and review-cycle counts were not needed to
make the terminal decision and do not independently prove task-boundary

## Meaning Of The Decision

`proceed_to_design` means only that a separately approved Tier 2 proposal may
design bounded, user-approved Task decomposition. It does not create that
proposal or Task. It does not justify automatic self-splitting, automatic Task
creation, parent/child runtime behavior, or any present change to the Skill,
CLI, SQLite schema, Viewer, network boundary, or target-project mutation
boundary.

## Limitations

- The result covers one repository, one expansion scenario, one paired trial,
  and one model/tool/permission cohort. It is not population-level causal
  evidence.
- Observer and reviewer labels record process separation but do not
  authenticate a person, model, provider identity, or organizational
  independence.
- The measurements show a difference under the frozen protocol; they do not
  determine the eventual design, threshold, user interaction, or failure
  policy.
- The study does not answer whether an LLM should autonomously discover and
  create successor Tasks. That remains outside the observed behavior.
- Raw prompts, chats, reasoning, reviews, Task prose, diffs, paths, command
  streams, credentials, and provider payloads were outside retention. Local
  deletion does not claim deletion of provider-side service records.

## Retirement And No-Rerun Boundary

After the one allowed reduction and terminal decision, trial roots, controls,
candidate records, journal, reduced corpus, protocol, episode plan, temporary
harness, and dedicated tests were removed. The installable package tree was
unchanged by the study.

Only
`fixtures/m20s/decomposition-collection-receipt.json` remains as a bounded
terminal tombstone. It records corpus identity, size, aggregate counts,
decision, and the exact retirement anchor after finalization. It contains
insufficient material to reconstruct, rerun, or rescore the study and never
satisfies a current verification, review, completion, or release gate.
